Verification
The release preflight and execution logs are the authoritative current status.
Regression tests include nonfinite inputs, coverage eligibility, missing models,
extra/duplicate keys, interrupted downloads and unpadded source dates. The final
source pipeline must finish before packaging; the report is rendered for visual

Human requirements still depend on supplied information
- Actual names, roll numbers and contribution details.
- Confirmation that this dataset matches CAL 1, or instructor approval.
- Merging with the actual prior report when it is supplied.
These are not replaced by a fabricated identity or approval checkbox.

Research limitations retained honestly
Historical one-household data has no verified natural fault ground truth.
Controlled bursts do not validate real faults. Final snapshot accuracy does not
measure detection latency. A graceful restart does not test crash-during-write
failure. Local timings do not establish distributed scaling. Broader baselines,
labelled multi-household data, uncertainty estimates and multi-executor studies
remain the publication plan, rather than unsupported completed claims.
