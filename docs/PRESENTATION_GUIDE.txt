4:00-6:00  Structured Streaming (CO4, 2 marks)
Show outputs/evidence.html#stream and the live UI.
"Files arrive atomically. Spark reads a declared schema, rejects nonfinite input,
tracks event time with a watermark and aggregates one-hour windows. Each batch
writes updated results using a stable batch ID. We restart from the same
checkpoint halfway through and compare the latest windows to a batch reference."
"A window below 54 readings has an insufficient_coverage status and a null
alert. A false alert would mean we scored it and found no threshold crossing.
Those statements must remain distinct. Window finality is a separate column."
Explain the 0/30/180-minute probe and 2/72/168-hour real delayed replays.
"Longer watermarks retain more evidence for this delay schedule and keep more
state. They are not universally optimal settings."

Viva answers worth rehearsing
- Why Spark here? The assignment requires the APIs and execution model. This
  dataset fits a laptop; Spark overhead may exceed pandas for small workloads.
- Why not groupByKey? reduceByKey combines locally before shuffling.
- Why no interpolation? Filling from future observations can leak information
  and distort evaluation; observed energy and coverage remain explicit.
- Is source time UTC? No offset is supplied. A fixed process/SQL zone preserves
  source wall-clock values consistently; true UTC instants remain unresolved.
- Is every late row dropped? No. Admission depends on watermark and window state.
- Is a watermark wall-clock time? No, it follows maximum observed event time.
- Why hour-of-week in streaming if global wins overall? It is a preselected
  inspectable demonstrator. Test-based model replacement would consume holdout.
- Does checkpoint restart prove crash recovery? It proves a graceful restart in
  this experiment. Crash-during-write behavior remains a separate test.
- Does overwrite by batch ID make any sink exactly-once? No. It handles local
  single-writer retries for this output design; arbitrary sinks need transactions.
- Are the 54 cases independent? No. They are 18 shared scenarios x three models.
- Why weak F1? Short/weak injected changes overlap ordinary household variation;
  aggregation and threshold calibration also affect sensitivity. Use the actual
  scenario results and avoid inventing a causal explanation.
- Is this a new detector or guaranteed paper? No. The report states the research
  limits and a concrete publication plan.
